Check-in and Check-out
Check-in time is 3:00 PM. Check-out time is 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late
check-out are subject to availability and may incur an additional fee of $25.

Pet Policy
We welcome pets under 30 lbs for an additional fee of $50 per stay. A maximum
of two pets is allowed per room. Service animals are always welcome at no charge.

Cancellation Policy
Reservations can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before the
check-in date. Cancellations made within 48 hours of check-in will be charged
one night's stay.

Parking
Self-parking is available for $20 per night. Valet parking is available for
$35 per night. Both options include in-and-out privileges.

Wi-Fi
Complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the hotel, including
all guest rooms and common areas. No password is required in public areas;
guest room Wi-Fi passwords are provided at check-in.

Pool and Gym Hours
The rooftop pool is open daily from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM. The fitness center
is open 24 hours for registered guests using their room key.
